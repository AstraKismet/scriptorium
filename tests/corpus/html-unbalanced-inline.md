Unbalanced inline markup is ordinary input, not an error.

A stray < in prose, an <b>unclosed tag, and an orphan </i> whose partner sits
in another block.

Mismatched <b>emphasis</i> must not pair with the wrong partner, and a void
<br> element must not shadow the <b>pair</b> around it.

A self-closing <img src="cover.png" /> is standalone whatever its name says.
